Welcome to Dragon Repeller. Defeat the dragon to help the townspeople leave. You are in the town square. Where will you go?

ⓘ How to play and quick tips

Play guide

  1. Visit the store to buy health or a stronger weapon.
  2. Enter the cave and defeat monsters to earn XP and gold.
  3. Return to town and choose Fight dragon when ready.

Controls and tips

Choose an action with a tap or click. On a keyboard, use Tab to move and Enter or Space to choose.

Momentum counts hits in a row. It resets when you miss and does not change damage.

Tip: Upgrade your weapon before you challenge the dragon.
